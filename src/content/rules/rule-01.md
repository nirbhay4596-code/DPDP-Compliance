## What it says

The Rules are called the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025. They commence in three stages:

- Rules 1, 2 and 17 to 21 — on publication in the Official Gazette.
- Rule 4 — one year after publication.
- Rules 3, 5 to 16, 22 and 23 — eighteen months after publication.

## The Act commences in exactly the same three stages

Two notifications were issued the same day — G.S.R. 843(E) for the Act, G.S.R. 846(E) for the Rules. They are deliberately in lockstep.

| Stage | Date | Act sections | Rules |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 13 Nov 2025 | ss. 2, 18–26, 35–43, 44(1),(3) | 1, 2, 17–21 |

| 3 | 13 May 2027 | ss. 3–5, 6(1)–(8),(10), 7–17, 27–34, 36, 37 | 3, 5–16, 22, 23 |

## The situation

Meera's nephew says the law is already in force. Her accountant says next year. Her cousin says 2025. They are all partly right.

## What you must do

Understand what the stages mean. Stage 1 — already live — is entirely about building the regulator: appointing its Chairperson, paying them, how it meets and votes. Nothing in stage 1 burdens a business.

Everything that costs you money lands together on **13 May 2027**.

## What if that feels like plenty of time?

It isn't, for three reasons.

1. **A year of security logs is an engineering project.** Most systems keep 30 to 90 days. Changing that costs money and takes planning.
2. **Breach reporting needs a rehearsed drill.** You must send the regulator a detailed report within 72 hours. That runbook has to exist before the incident.
3. **Old consents must be refreshed** — s.5(2). No transition relief. For a large user base, months of work.

## What if the date is actually the 14th?

The notification is dated 13 November 2025; the Gazette PDF is digitally signed 14 November 2025. Commencement is keyed to "the date of publication".

At the boundary, treat 13 November as the conservative date for obligations on you, and 14 November for rights you assert. A single day will rarely matter — but the Third Schedule clock ("or the commencement of these Rules, whichever is latest") does turn on it.
